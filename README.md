# CircleScreen App



CircleScreen is the companion mobile app for a custom circular display which allows users to upload and download images to their CircleScreens. It combines a variety of elements outlined throughout this README. Currently, this app is available for download on the iOS app store, [here](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/circlescreen-uploader/id6757413972)!



## Current Features

- Upload photos from your phone gallery to your CircleScreen

- Download/Remove photos from from your CircleScreen gallery



We're still developing this little device, more features to come! Currently, the screens randomly cycle through the images in their gallery. 



## Hardware

- XH-C2HX BLE-enabled Microcontroller

- 1.28" TFT LCD Display Module (Round)

- usb-c module



## Circuitry


We engineered the circuitry behind the screen using a mixture of KiCAD for pcb design, and FreeCAD for the machining side of things (using our CNC router).
